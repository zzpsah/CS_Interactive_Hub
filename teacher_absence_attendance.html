Salary Rules & Official Gazette PDF

Notification 786 · Gazette 274 · 15 March 2024. Rule 13A: Annexure-A Fitment Matrix. Rule 13B: promotion/upgradation. Annual increment reference: Finance Department Resolution No. 3590 dated 24 May 2017; increment dates are 1 January / 1 July, with no more than one increment in a year as applicable to the verified service record.

Basic payUses the BPSC fitment values for IX–X or XI–XII based on the imported class category.
Increment estimateUses service joining date and “Basic pay as of” to count annual steps on the applicable Jan/Jul schedule.
Manual verificationPromotion, upgradation, senior-category placement, breaks in service and special orders are not inferred automatically.

Monthly calculation settings are in the top toolbar: DA, HRA, NPS, medical allowance, GIS and working days. Income tax remains manually entered per teacher. Changing a rate recalculates the dashboard and statement.
